**Date:** 2026-11-06 | **Unit:** Articles & Prepositions
- **Prompt:** Self-introduction expressing career experience and seeking remote work without incorrect indefinite articles.
- **Your Answer:** "I have five years of experience... looking for a new remote work." (2026-11-06)
- **Correct Answer:** "looking for remote work" / "looking for a remote role / job"
- **Why:** Spanish Interference Trap. In Spanish, "un trabajo remoto" is countable. In English, "work" is uncountable. Never "a remote work". Either use zero article ("looking for remote work") or a countable noun ("a remote position / role / job").
- **Next Review:** 2026-11-07
- **Streak:** 0

**Date:** 2026-11-06 | **Unit:** Advanced Phrasal Verbs
- **Prompt:** Asking a lead to review work using "run it by".
- **Your Answer:** "Could you run it by you and give me your feedback?" (2026-11-06)
- **Correct Answer:** "Could I run it by you?" / "I wanted to run it by you before the meeting."
- **Why:** Subject/Object pronoun inversion. The person who has the idea/work is the subject (*I* run it by *you*). You cannot ask another person to run something by themselves ("Could you run it by you").
