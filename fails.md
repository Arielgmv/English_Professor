# Failure Pile
Everything I didn't get right on the first try. It's not a punishment: it's the exact list of what I still need to learn. Maintained by The Professor.

**The Rule:** What I fail returns the next day, and keeps returning until I get it right twice in a row on different days. If I fail it again, the streak resets to zero. With two consecutive correct answers, it leaves the pile and moves to `reviews.md`.
Getting it right with a hint also counts as a failure.

## In the Pile

**Date:** 2026-11-06 | **Unit:** Articles & Prepositions
- **Prompt:** Self-introduction expressing career experience and seeking remote work without incorrect indefinite articles.
- **Your Answer:** "I have five years of experience... looking for a new remote work." (2026-11-06); "I am currently prioritizing remote work..." (2026-11-09).
- **Correct Answer:** "looking for remote work" / "prioritizing remote work" / "looking for a remote role / job"
- **Why:** Spanish Interference Trap. In Spanish, "un trabajo remoto" is countable. In English, "work" is uncountable. Never "a remote work". Successfully produced zero-article "remote work" on 2026-11-09.

**Date:** 2026-11-06 | **Unit:** Advanced Phrasal Verbs
- **Prompt:** Asking a lead to review work using "run it by".
- **Your Answer:** "Could you run it by you and give me your feedback?" (2026-11-06); "before I publish this post-mortem SOP... I'd like to run it by you" (2026-11-09).
- **Correct Answer:** "Could I run it by you?" / "I'd like to run it by you..."
- **Why:** Subject/Object pronoun inversion. The person who has the idea/work is the subject (*I* run it by *you*). Successfully fixed syntax on 2026-11-09.
