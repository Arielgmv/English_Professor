## I. The Core Problem: Why Spanish Speakers Struggle

### 1.1 The Invisible Words
**Problem:** Spanish doesn't use articles the same way English does. You omit them where English requires them, and vice versa.

**Literal Translation Trap:**
- ❌ "I have experience in marketing" (missing article)
- ✅ "I have **experience** in marketing" (uncountable = no article)
- ✅ "I have **an experience** that changed me" (countable = needs article)

**Rule:** If you can count it (one experience, two experiences), you need an article. If it's abstract/uncountable, often no article.

### 1.2 Prepositions Don't Translate
**Problem:** You're translating "en" → "in/on/at" without learning the collocation.

**Examples from your diagnostic:**
- ❌ "depend **of**" (literal Spanish: depender de)
- ✅ "depend **on**"
- ❌ "interested **for**" (literal Spanish: interesado por)
- ✅ "interested **in**"

## II. Articles: The Decision Tree

### 2.1 The Three Questions (Ask in Order)

**Question 1: Is it countable or uncountable?**
- Countable: book, idea, person, meeting, error
- Uncountable: information, advice, knowledge, experience (when general), marketing

**Question 2: Is it specific or general?**
- Specific: THE book on my desk, THE meeting we had yesterday
- General: A book (any book), A meeting (any meeting)

**Question 3: First mention or already mentioned?**
- First time: A/AN
- Second time+: THE

### 2.2 Common C1 Traps

**Trap 1: Abstract Nouns**
- ❌ "I need **the** more information"
- ✅ "I need **more** information" (uncountable, no article)
- ✅ "I need **the** information you promised" (specific = THE)

**Trap 2: Jobs and Roles**
- ❌ "I work as **the** engineer"
- ✅ "I work as **an** engineer" (one of many engineers)
- ✅ "I am **the** engineer on this project" (specific role = THE)

**Trap 3: Institutions (British vs American)**
- ❌ "I went to **the** university" (if you're a student)
- ✅ "I went to **university**" (UK: no article for institution purpose)
- ✅ "I went to **the** university" (if visiting the building)

**Trap 4: Superlatives and Ordinals**
- Always THE: "the best candidate," "the third time," "the most important"

### 2.3 Zero Article Cases (When NOT to use A/AN/THE)

1. **Plural generalizations:**
   - ✅ "Engineers solve problems" (all engineers in general)
   - ❌ "The engineers solve problems" (specific engineers only)

2. **Meals:**
   - ✅ "I had breakfast"
   - ❌ "I had the breakfast" (unless: "the breakfast you made")

3. **Languages:**
   - ✅ "I speak Spanish"
   - ❌ "I speak the Spanish"

4. **Sports/Activities:**
   - ✅ "I play football"
   - ❌ "I play the football"

## III. Prepositions: The Collocation Method

### 3.1 Don't Learn Prepositions Alone
**Wrong approach:** Memorizing "in = en, on = en, at = en"
**Right approach:** Learn the whole phrase

**Examples:**
- ❌ "I'm good **in** English"
- ✅ "I'm good **at** English" (collocation: good AT)
- ❌ "It depends **of** the situation"
- ✅ "It depends **on** the situation" (collocation: depend ON)

### 3.2 Time Prepositions: The Funnel Method

**AT** → Specific point (narrowest)
- at 9:30, at noon, at the moment, at Christmas

**ON** → Days/dates (medium)
- on Monday, on January 15, on my birthday, on the weekend (US)

**IN** → Longer periods (widest)
- in 2026, in January, in the morning, in the 21st century, in 5 minutes

### 3.3 Place Prepositions: The Container Method

**IN** → Inside a 3D space
- in the room, in Bolivia, in the box, in the world

**ON** → Surface/line
- on the table, on the wall, on the page, on the left

**AT** → Specific point/location
- at the door, at the bus stop, at work, at home, at the meeting

### 3.4 C1 Business Collocations (Priority for Remote Work)

**Verbs + Prepositions:**
- apply **for** a job
- specialize **in** automation
- collaborate **with** a team
- report **to** a manager
- focus **on** results
- deal **with** clients
- succeed **in** a role

**Adjectives + Prepositions:**
- responsible **for** tasks
- interested **in** learning
- familiar **with** tools
- capable **of** handling
- excited **about** opportunities
- qualified **for** the position

## IV. Exercises (Pre-Test Before Theory)

### 4.1 Diagnostic: Articles
**Fill in: a/an/the/Ø (zero article)**

1. I work as ___ engineer at ___ tech company.
2. ___ information you sent was helpful.
3. She has ___ MBA from ___ university in ___ UK.
4. I need ___ advice about ___ career change.
5. He's ___ best candidate for ___ position.

### 4.2 Diagnostic: Prepositions
**Fill in: in/on/at/for/with/about/of**

1. I'm interested ___ learning Python.
2. This depends ___ the client's requirements.
3. She's responsible ___ managing the team.
4. We'll meet ___ Monday ___ 2:30 PM.
5. I'm not familiar ___ that tool.

### 4.3 Real-World Application
**Correct the errors (from typical Spanish speaker mistakes):**

1. "I have the experience in automation and I'm looking for a work in remote."
2. "The Monday I have a meeting in the office at 9:00."
3. "I'm good in English but I need to improve in the grammar."
4. "I depend of the team to finish the project in time."
5. "She has a MBA and she's the expert in marketing."

### 4.4 Answer Key

**4.1 Articles:**
1. an / a
2. The
3. an / a / the
4. Ø / a
5. the / the

**4.2 Prepositions:**
1. in
2. on
3. for
4. on / at
5. with

**4.3 Real-World Application:**
1. "I have **Ø** experience in automation and I'm looking for **Ø** remote work."
2. "**Ø** Monday I have a meeting in the office at 9:00."
3. "I'm good **at** English but I need to improve **Ø** grammar."
4. "I depend **on** the team to finish the project in time."
5. "She has **an** MBA and she's **an** expert in marketing."

## V. Common Error Patterns (Your Personal Triggers)

### 5.1 From Your Diagnostic
**Pattern 1:** Using "the" before uncountable nouns
- ❌ "the information," "the advice," "the knowledge"
- ✅ "information," "advice," "knowledge" (unless specific: "the information you sent")

**Pattern 2:** Wrong preposition after adjectives
- ❌ "good in," "interested for," "capable to"
- ✅ "good at," "interested in," "capable of"

**Pattern 3:** Article with jobs
- ❌ "I work as the engineer"
- ✅ "I work as an engineer"

### 5.2 The "Spanish Interference" Checklist
Before writing/speaking, check:
- [ ] Did I just translate "en" directly? (Could be in/on/at)
- [ ] Did I use "the" before an abstract noun? (Often wrong)
- [ ] Did I say "good in"? (Should be "good at")
- [ ] Did I say "depend of"? (Should be "depend on")

## VI. Practice Framework

### 6.1 Daily Drill (5 minutes)
**Method:** Write 3 sentences about your day using:
1. One article correctly (a/an/the/zero)
2. One preposition collocation (interested in, good at, etc.)
3. One time expression (at 9:30, on Monday, in October)

**Example:**
- "I had **a** productive meeting **at** 10:00 **on** Monday."
- "I'm excited **about** **the** new project starting **in** November."

### 6.2 Weekly Challenge
**Task:** Record yourself describing your work experience (2 minutes). Then transcribe it and check:
- Articles before jobs/roles
- Prepositions after adjectives
- Time/place prepositions

**Target:** Zero errors from your "Common Error Patterns" list.

## VII. Quick Reference Tables

### Articles Decision Tree

```
Is it countable?
├─ NO (uncountable: information, advice) → Ø (zero article)
│   └─ Unless specific → THE
└─ YES
    ├─ First mention/any one → A/AN
    └─ Specific/already mentioned → THE
```

### Prepositions: Time
| Preposition | Use | Examples |
|-------------|-----|----------|
| AT | Point | at 9:00, at noon, at the moment |
| ON | Day/Date | on Monday, on Jan 15, on my birthday |
| IN | Period | in 2026, in January, in the morning, in 5 minutes |

### Prepositions: Place
| Preposition | Use | Examples |
|-------------|-----|----------|
| IN | Inside/enclosed | in the room, in Bolivia, in the box |
| ON | Surface | on the table, on the wall, on the page |
| AT | Point/location | at the door, at work, at the meeting |
