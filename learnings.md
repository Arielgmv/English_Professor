## Recurring Patterns
When the same type of error appears three times, it is noted here. This is the most valuable part of the file.
- **Spanish Interference Trap (Prepositions):** Translating prepositions word-for-word from Spanish (e.g., "depends of", "good in", "at the morning", "in the night") instead of learning the English collocation as a single block.
- **Job vs. Work Interference Trap (Uncountable Confusion):** Treating *work* as countable (*"a remote work"*) or *job* as uncountable (*"a lot of job"*, *"continue with my job"*). Rule: *Job* = countable specific role/position; *Work* = uncountable activity/tasks ([Murphy Unit 70A](file:///home/ariel/Documents/Obsidian_Notes/Notes/English_Professor/murphy-index-v2.md#L37)).

## ⭐ STAR Story Bank (#star-stories)

*Goal: Polished, memorized narratives for behavioral interviews.*

### Theme: Handling Difficult Clients

- **Situation:** A key client was threatening to churn due to a recurring API timeout issue.

- **Task:** I needed to de-escalate the situation, explain the technical root cause without jargon, and provide an immediate workaround.

- **Action:** I scheduled a call, acknowledged their frustration using empathetic mirroring, explained the issue as a "traffic jam" in our server, and manually routed their requests to a backup node.

- **Result:** The client not only stayed but upgraded their plan the following month, citing our transparent communication.
